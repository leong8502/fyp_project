TalentSync is a Malaysian freelance marketplace platform built to bridge the gap between skilled freelancers and clients who need quality work — powered by AI matching technology.

Based in Malaysia AI-Powered Matching Secure Escrow Payments

Our Mission

To empower Malaysian businesses and freelancers by providing a trustworthy, intelligent, and accessible platform that makes hiring and working seamless, fair, and rewarding for everyone involved.

Our Vision

To become Malaysia's leading freelance platform — where every talented individual can find meaningful work, and every business can find the right person to bring their ideas to life.

Platform Features

Why TalentSync is Different

AI-Powered Matching

Our intelligent algorithm analyses project requirements and freelancer profiles to surface the best matches — saving both parties time and effort.

Escrow Payment Protection

Funds are held securely in escrow and only released once the client approves deliverables. Freelancers get paid on time, every time.

Real-Time Messaging

Integrated chat lets clients and freelancers communicate directly within the platform, keeping everything in one place.

Verified Reviews

Every completed project can be reviewed. Build your reputation and let your work speak for itself on your public profile.

Integrated Wallet

Both clients and freelancers have a digital wallet for seamless top-ups, withdrawals, and transaction history tracking.

Smart Dashboard

A clean, intuitive dashboard gives you full visibility of your projects, bids, earnings, and communications at a glance.
